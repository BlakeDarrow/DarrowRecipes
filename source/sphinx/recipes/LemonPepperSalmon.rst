Lemon Pepper Salmon
###########################################################
 
Ingredients
=========================================================
 
- Salmon
- Olive oil
- Lemon pepper combo seasoning 
 
Prep
=========================================================
 
1. Totally dry the salmon with a paper towel on both sides
2. Pre-heat oven to 400 or so
 
Directions
=========================================================
 
1. Thoroughly rub in olive oil over side of salmon
2. Run in lemon pepper seasoning
3. Repeat to other side
4. Cook in oven for 14-16 minutes until inside is 125-130*
